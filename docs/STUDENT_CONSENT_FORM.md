# Disheera Student Pilot Informed Consent & Data Governance Agreement
**Institution:** G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering and Management (GHRCEM), Jalgaon  
**Program:** 8-Week Placement Readiness & Verified Proof-of-Work Pilot (Batch of 2026)  
**Governing Standard:** Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (Self-Assessed Compliance)  
**Document Revision:** 1.0 (Pre-Public Release)

---

## 1. Purpose of the Pilot
You are invited to participate in an academic placement-readiness study conducted jointly by the G.H. Raisoni College of Engineering and Management (GHRCEM) Training & Placement Cell (TPO) and Disheera Technologies Inc.

The objective of this pilot is to evaluate whether **two-factor verified proof of work** (curated code milestone challenges + code-grounded Voice Viva interrogation + tamper-proof Skill Passports) increases corporate recruiter shortlisting rates compared to traditional static 1-page PDF resumes.

---

## 2. What Data Is Collected (Data Minimization Principle)
Under the strict principle of data minimization, Disheera collects **only professional, learning, and skill-verification data**:

| Category | Specific Data Points | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identity & Academic Context** | Full legal name, college email address, branch/major, academic year, college name. | Verification of institutional enrollment and cohort segmentation. |
| **Communication** | Mobile number (1:1 partial unique index for OTP auth & WhatsApp milestone nudges). | Preventing duplicate accounts and delivering milestone alerts. |
| **Technical Portfolio** | Public GitHub handle, repository metadata, commit diffs, AST code structure. | Evaluation of hands-on code authenticity for skill calibration. |
| **Integrity Telemetry** | Milestone quiz response velocity, proctor audit flags (tab switches, full-screen exits), Voice Viva audio response recordings. | Anti-cheat certification and issuance of verifiable cryptographic passport seals. |

### What Is NEVER Collected:
- ❌ **No Government IDs:** Aadhaar, PAN card, Voter ID, or Passport documents are **never** requested or stored.
- ❌ **No Financial Information:** No bank account numbers, UPI IDs, or credit/debit card information.
- ❌ **No Invasive Device Surveillance:** Zero background keystroke logging, screen recording outside the assessment chamber, or web history tracking.

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Used
1. **Personalized Roadmaps:** Calibrating customized 8-week learning milestones based on your initial diagnostic assessment.
2. **Skill Passport Generation:** Compiling verified milestones, GitHub AST analysis, and Voice Viva scores into a public, tamper-proof digital passport (`https://disheera.site/passport.html?candidate=<id>`) protected by SHA-256 seals.
3. **Placement Cell Telemetry:** Providing GHRCEM TPO with real-time cohort progression analytics, at-risk flags (inactivity > 7 days), and placement-ready rosters for visiting corporate recruiters.
4. **Recruiter Discovery:** Verified corporate recruiters who have passed DNS MX-record domain vetting can view your verified Skill Passport and invite you to interview pipelines.

---

## 4. Voluntary Participation & Opt-In Granularity
Your participation in this pilot is **100% voluntary**. You have the right to select your data sharing preferences:

- **[Option A] Full Pilot Participation (Recommended):**  
  Participate in weekly roadmaps, earn Skill Passport seals, and have your verified profile shared with vetted corporate recruiters visiting GHRCEM.
- **[Option B] Learning-Only Mode:**  
  Participate in free learning roadmaps, assessments, and coding projects without sharing your data with external corporate recruiters.

---

## 5. Right to Withdraw & Data Erasure (DPDP Act 2023)
You retain sovereign ownership of your personal data at all times:
- **Right to Withdraw:** You may withdraw from the pilot at any time without penalty or impact on your academic standing, grades, or internal college credits.
- **Right to Erasure (Forget Me):** You can request the permanent deletion of your account, quiz attempts, audio recordings, and Skill Passport by emailing `privacy@disheera.site` or using the account deletion workflow. All associated records will be purged from active databases within 72 hours.
- **Data Retention Period:** Inactive accounts are retained for the duration of the 8-week pilot plus 6 months for academic benchmark validation, after which personal identifying information is irreversibly anonymized.
