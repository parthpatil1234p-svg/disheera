# Disheera — HyperFrames Video Storylines

Three distinct storylines for a 30–35 second product launch & explainer film showcasing Disheera, an Autonomous Career GPS and Institutional Verification platform.

---

## Storyline A (Recommended) — "The Verified Milestone"
> **Why recommended:** Directly showcases Disheera's core differentiator: turning student assessment outcomes into cryptographically verified hiring signals that recruiters instantly act upon.

- **Device:** One object across apps (a verified skill badge traveling through student & recruiter workflows)
- **Form:** Object-to-object chain (each shot ends on the element that triggers the next)
- **Summary:** A student completes a diagnostic sprint on Disheera; their verified 94.2% score seals into a cryptographic Career Passport badge, which instantly animates onto a Fortune 500 recruiter's talent discovery radar as an unlocked dossier. The viewer experiences momentum and unshakeable trust—seeing how diagnostic rigor directly unlocks high-tier employment.
- **First 2 Seconds:** Full-bleed punch-in on a diagnostic code assessment timer hitting `00:00`, a green badge animates with `Diagnostic Verified: Top 3.8%`, and the camera snaps back as the cryptographic hash seals.
- **Beats:** Hook (assessment timer & score verification) → Tension (recruiter drowning in 500+ unverified PDF resumes) → Reveal (*Disheera: Autonomous Career GPS & Institutional Verification*) → Proof (Interactive Career Roadmap sprint cleared → ATS LaTeX resume compiles with 96/100 score → Recruiter Radar matches verified talent) → Payoff (Candidate Dossier instant unlock + WhatsApp interview invite) → Claim (*From Diagnostic Rigor to Direct Recruiter Unlock*) → End Card (Disheera lockup, royal blue `#2563EB`, URL: `disheera.site`).
- **Look:** Clean EduTech Light Theme (`#F8FAFC` canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#0F172A` Inter type at 32px/48px, `#2563EB` Royal Blue accent, `#10B981` verification seals, 0px dark neon).
- **Sound:** Snappy tactile interface clicks, crisp glass seal chime on cryptographic verification, uptempo 118 BPM modern rhythmic track.

## Storyline B — "The Talent Signal"
- **Device:** Life before and after (the chaos of unverified hiring vs. autonomous verification)
- **Form:** Split screen to one (two parallel pains converging into one clean solution)
- **Summary:** Left screen shows a college student submitting 40+ unread generic job applications; right screen shows a recruiter squinting through 800 unverified resumes with skepticism. The split screen snaps and collapses into Disheera's clean candidate cockpit where verified skills, institutional attestation, and WhatsApp alerts bring both together in 48 hours. The viewer feels relief and clarity.
- **First 2 Seconds:** Split screen: left side rapidly scrolling an applicant tracking rejection email; right side showing a red "Unverified Claim" warning on a PDF resume, cutting hard on beat 2.
- **Beats:** Hook (Split-screen hiring frustration) → Tension (85% of college resumes contain unverified claims) → Reveal (*Disheera: Verified Career Infrastructure*) → Proof (Student completes assessment chamber → Roadmap unlocks next sprint → Recruiter filters by verified benchmark) → Payoff (Both sides meet on Recruiter Cockpit with instant WhatsApp invitation) → Claim (*The End of Unverified Hiring*) → End Card (Disheera seal & get started button).
- **Look:** Muted slate `#64748B` split panes collapsing into high-contrast `#FFFFFF` cards with `#2563EB` highlights and `#10B981` verified badges on `#F8FAFC` canvas.
- **Sound:** Discordant typewriter clatter on the hook resolving into a clean bass drop and energetic synth groove.

---

## Storyline C — "Autonomous GPS Flight"
- **Device:** The feature as the hook (the living, recalculating Career GPS)
- **Form:** A flight through one world (continuous gliding camera across a topological career roadmap)
- **Summary:** The camera glides through a living "Career GPS" map where each semester is an active sector. As the camera flies forward, skill gaps illuminate in amber, assessment sprints turn them green, and the roadmap recalculates live like turn-by-turn navigation routing around obstacles. The viewer feels empowerment, orientation, and structured velocity.
- **First 2 Seconds:** Macro camera dive straight into a topological node labeled `Current Readiness: 62%`, instantly sweeping along an illuminated blue route as a sprint completes and the readiness score surges to `88.5%`.
- **Beats:** Hook (Macro dive into live dynamic Career GPS route) → Tension (Career confusion: "What do I learn next?") → Reveal (*Disheera Autonomous Career GPS*) → Proof (Sprint 1 Frontend cleared → Sprint 2 System Architecture unlocked → AI Resume Studio compiles LaTeX PDF) → Payoff (16:9 Verifiable Certificate generated with QR verification) → Claim (*Your Career. Navigated.*) → End Card (Disheera logo, tagline, and `Explore Your Route`).
- **Look:** Clean architectural white `#FFFFFF` surface with light blue `#EFF6FF` route grid, `#0F172A` bold display numerals, `#2563EB` animated path lines.
- **Sound:** Ambient cinematic whoosh into crisp rhythm track with melodic marimba pulses.

## Assumptions
- **Runtime:** 30–35 seconds (900–1050 frames @ 30 fps)
- **Format:** 1920×1080 (16:9 landscape)
- **Audio:** High-energy instrumental electronic/percussive background track with UI sound design (100% free royalty-free / CC0, zero paid assets)
- **Voiceover:** No voiceover required (text and live product interface carry the narrative); optional AI narration available via free TTS if requested.
