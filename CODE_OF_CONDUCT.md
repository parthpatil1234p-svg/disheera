# Code of Conduct — Team 404 Brain Not Found

> **Hack2Ignite 2026–27 · CareerPath AI**

## 🤝 Our Commitment

We, as members of **Team 404 Brain Not Found**, pledge to foster a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment throughout the Hack2Ignite hackathon. We are dedicated to providing a constructive experience for every team member and participant, regardless of age, background, identity, skill level, or technological experience.

---

## 🌟 Expected Standards

- **Respect & Inclusivity:** Value differing viewpoints, communicate constructively, and show empathy toward fellow teammates.
- **Academic & Hackathon Integrity:** Maintain 100% originality. Never plagiarize code, commit unauthorized proprietary materials, or copy assets without proper open-source licensing.
- **Responsible Collaboration:** Keep code branches updated, communicate blockers proactively, and uphold shared commitments to the project timeline.
- **Constructive Code Reviews:** Critique code and design decisions with positive intent and actionable feedback.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

- Offensive comments, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Committing unauthorized, copied, or malicious code to the repository.
- Deliberately misleading team members or judges regarding project progress or testing results.

---

## 🛡️ Reporting & Enforcement

If an issue arises or conduct violates this standard:
1. Address the situation privately with the teammate if appropriate.
2. If unresolved, escalate privately to **Parth Patil (Team Leader)**.
3. If necessary, issues will be brought to Hack2Ignite hackathon organizers for mediation.

Unacceptable behavior will result in a warning, task reassignment, or removal from the hackathon team roster in accordance with event rules.
