Frequently Asked Questions

What is Disheera and how does it help college students?

Disheera is an autonomous AI Career GPS and verifiable talent operating system. It evaluates engineering and collegiate students through adaptive diagnostic tests, pinpoints skill gaps against industry benchmarks, creates structured step-by-step roadmaps, and provides oral AI viva defenses to prove code authenticity to recruiters.

How does the Oral AI Viva Defense work?

When a student submits project milestones or completes weekly learning roadmaps, Disheera's oral AI examiner conducts a live, conversational viva defense. The AI queries the student on code decisions, trade-offs, edge-case handling, and architectural choices, ensuring that the submitted work reflects authentic knowledge rather than copied code.

How do recruiters verify student credentials on Disheera?

Every completed milestone, assessment, and certification on Disheera receives a cryptographic SHA-256 certificate ID. Corporate recruiters and university faculties can verify credential validity instantly via the public verification registry at https://disheera.site/verify without needing login credentials.

Is Disheera free for students and job seekers?

Yes, Disheera provides completely free access to core career diagnostic assessments, personalized learning roadmaps, resume building tools, and public credential verification for all students and job seekers.
