🏛️ Chart 3: The 4-Pillar Temple of Industry Job Readiness
Strict Objective Standards for Digital Credential Issuance

In the spirit of national standardisation, DheePath mandates that students satisfy all four objective gates before earning a verified Job-Ready digital certificate:
JobReady = (Score ≥ 70%) ∧ (VerifiedSkills ≥ 4) ∧ (RoadmapProgress ≥ 80%) ∧ (ExpiredSkills = 0)

📐
PILLAR 1
Score ≥ 70%
Deterministic 60/25/15 calculation combines verified skill proficiency, personal interests, and academic baseline.
💎
PILLAR 2
Verified Skills ≥ 4
Must have at least 4 role-critical core skills backed by GitHub AST Code badges or Groq Quiz badges.
📈
PILLAR 3
Progress ≥ 80%
Must complete minimum 80% of milestone roadmap tasks with anti-scrubbing reflection syntheses.
⏱️
PILLAR 4
Expired Skills = 0
Enforces a 90-day retention half-life to ensure technical currency before entering the recruiter radar.
