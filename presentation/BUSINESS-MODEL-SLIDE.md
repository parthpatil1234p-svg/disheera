## 🗣️ Speaker Notes & Delivery Script (45 Seconds)

> **[Slide Intro — 0:00 - 0:10]**  
> *"Judges always ask how CareerPath AI makes money if we don't charge students. Our answer is simple: **We keep our platform 100% free for students, always.** Charging students creates friction and kills viral adoption, while our mission is democratizing career guidance."*

> **[B2B Pillars — 0:10 - 0:30]**  
> *"Instead, we operate a high-margin **B2B institutional model**:  
> First, **Colleges pay for our Placement Readiness Portal** — an annual SaaS license between ₹1.5 to ₹3 Lakhs. Placement Officers get batch-wide skill gap heatmaps and accreditation metrics essential for NAAC and NIRF rankings.  
> Second, **Companies pay for Verified Candidate Access**. Traditional recruitment agencies charge up to ₹80,000 per hire to sift through 1,500 spam applications. We give hiring partners pre-assessed, proof-of-work candidates filtered by exact 60/25/15 skill rubrics."*

> **[Wrap Up & Unit Economics — 0:30 - 0:45]**  
> *"Finally, we offer optional, strictly labeled certification partnerships with industry course providers. With 96% software gross margins, our 3-year model projects scaling from 12 pilot campuses to over ₹8 Crores in annual recurring revenue. Students learn for free, colleges improve placements, and employers hire with certainty."*

## 🎯 3 Key Bullet Points for the Slide
1. **Zero Student Friction:** 100% free access guarantees rapid campus adoption and high DAU engagement.
2. **Institutional Placement Intelligence:** TPOs pay ₹49,000 to ₹1,99,000/yr for real-time skill gap heatmaps and NAAC/NIRF reporting.
3. **Proof-of-Work Recruiter Marketplace:** Companies pay ₹24,999/mo to hire verified candidates, cutting screening costs by 60%.
